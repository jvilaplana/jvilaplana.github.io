About

I am a computer engineer and researcher at the Universitat de Lleida (UdL), Catalonia, where I teach and do research at the Escola Politècnica Superior as a full-time professor. I am a member of the Distributed Computing Group (GCD).

My work sits between distributed systems and data science. I study how to build and run computing infrastructure, from cloud to fog and edge, and how to use it for machine learning on real-world data, with a long-standing focus on applications in health.

Research interests

  • Cloud, fog and edge computing
  • Federated and distributed machine learning
  • Synthetic data and computer vision datasets
  • Trustworthy AI infrastructure
  • Data science and big data
  • eHealth and mobile health
